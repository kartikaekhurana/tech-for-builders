# NivaTech Workspace

Create a beautiful, minimal, premium one-page website for NivaTech, a modern technology procurement and workspace solutions company founded by Kartikae and Samit.

Brand

NivaTech

NivaTech helps startup founders, small businesses, student entrepreneurs and growing teams acquire reliable technology without the hassle of searching through refurbished markets themselves.

The company specializes in:

Refurbished and professionally tested laptops

Business laptops for founders and teams

Monitors and peripherals

Complete workspace setups

Technology procurement

Device testing and configuration

Small-team IT setup

The brand should feel like a modern technology partner, NOT a second-hand computer shop.

Brand positioning

NivaTech

Technology for ambitious beginnings.

Supporting line:

We source, test and set up the technology your team needs — so you can focus on building your business.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium, modern Indian startup aesthetic.

Visual inspiration:

Apple-level simplicity

Modern SaaS startup websites

Premium consulting firms

Minimal editorial layouts

Use:

Off-white / warm white background

Charcoal or near-black typography

One sophisticated accent colour

Large typography

Generous whitespace

Fine borders

Subtle shadows

Rounded corners used sparingly

Smooth micro-interactions

Subtle scroll animations

Do NOT use:

Loud gradients

Neon colours

Generic computer-store graphics

Excessive icons

Cheap-looking stock photos

Fake testimonials

Fake statistics

Fake client logos

E-commerce-style product grids

The site should feel trustworthy, sophisticated and founder-focused.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT DIRECTION

This is NOT an online laptop marketplace.

Do not create:

Shopping cart

Checkout

Product catalogue

Fake inventory

Fake pricing

Product comparison tables

The primary conversion is:

Founder → submits requirements → NivaTech recommends a solution.

The website should communicate:

"You tell us what your team needs. We figure out the hardware."

Make the final result feel like a credible early-stage technology company that could eventually grow into a full IT procurement and workspace-management business.
